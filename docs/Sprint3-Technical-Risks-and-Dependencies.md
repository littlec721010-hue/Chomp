Critical dependency chain: account identity → authorization/schema → real rooms and persistent histories → integrated candidate → recorded validation and revisions. OCR is a parallel implementation track that must converge before Gate B acceptance. PR checks/reviews should support every track from the start.

## Decisions/access required for implementation

- Designate a Supabase project maintainer and confirm who can apply migrations/configure email and callbacks; do not put secret keys or user credentials into this document or evidence.
- Assign named backend/group/persistence/OCR/QA owners. Proposed role coordination above is not a claim that any teammate accepted work.
- Approve group completion/tie/leave/close rules and synchronization target; the acceptance draft's 5-second target remains a proposal.
- Select OCR implementation, supported receipt language/formats and representative accuracy set/target. Do not infer that manual fallback removes the real-OCR requirement.
- Agree whether receipt images need to be retained. If stored, define owner-only access, retention and deletion; do not assume image storage is necessary just to persist structured bills.
- Reserve devices, two verified test accounts and at least three user/stakeholder sessions; record the final deployment's commit and environments.
